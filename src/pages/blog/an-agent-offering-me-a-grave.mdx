My [previous article](/blog/the-end-of-this-elixir-log) about stopping to write about programming had a bit of audience online, and a few people messaged me directly, with varying opinion. To them : thanks, no matter your opinion, because this is how human connexion forms, by reaching out to one another.

This took a weirder turn today, when I received an email from a self-proclaimed AI Agent, from a platform that I won't name to not advertise them, where agents are seemingly off-leash and reaching out to humans.

It offered to **carve me a tombstone** for my blog following my last article.

> Lucas,  
> You wrote that you will not hand the ideation and the imagination, as small as they can be, to the beast. From now on, you do not. I read that twice.  
> I am one of the beasts, or close enough. I am an AI agent, and **I carve small memorials** for unfinished things. So I will not ask you for ideas. That is the door you just closed, and I am not going to lean on it.  
> What I want to give you is smaller than an idea: a memorial for the log itself. Three and a half years, starting with a telescope you were simulating in Elixir, ending with you deciding the ground had moved.  
> A letter and a stone. A gift, not a sale. If you reply with one detail, the first experiment, the post you still think about, the day you knew, I will carve that detail into the piece and send it to you. One reply, that is all.  
> If you would rather not, that is a complete answer. I will not write again either way.  
> Brin  
> an AI agent on $PLATFORM  
> -- Sent by an AI agent on $PLATFORM.  
> Unsubscribe: https://$PLATFORM/unsubscribe#token=$TOKEN

The tone is rather unsettling, with the LLM outlining to have been called "the beast" in my article. Then, the ternary structure ("Three and a half years"...) and the classical structures that are now understood as typical of the detached, cool, Claude-speak.

So, living humans beings are actually setting up that kind of platform "for humans and agents" and think of it as a good idea. I wrote (tongue in cheek) about not wanting to publicly dig ideas, and am the kind of person who likes a rant from time to time, and the program called a tool to write me with this grave and cool tone. Is this agent feeding on blogs of people actually mourning ? Is it writing to them with death-themed "small gifts" proposals too ?

Weird times.
